Getting Here

Directions to Fernway by Stories

Fernway is in Mayaganahalli, Bangalore Mysore Highway, near Ramanagara — on the Bangalore–Mysore Highway (NH 275, Mysore Road) between Bidadi and Ramanagara.

From Bangalore

Head out of Bangalore city on Mysore Road towards Mysore. Continue past Bidadi and you'll reach Fernway at Mayaganahalli, shortly before Ramanagara — an easy weekend drive and a perfect first stop on a Bangalore–Mysore road trip.

From Mysore & Ramanagara

Driving towards Bangalore on the highway, pass Ramanagara and Fernway is a few minutes ahead at Mayaganahalli — a relaxed lunch or dinner stop before the final stretch into the city.

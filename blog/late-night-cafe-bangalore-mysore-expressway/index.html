Open Till 6am A Late-Night Cafe on the Bangalore–Mysore Expressway, Open Till 6am

24 November 2026

When the city closes, Fernway is just getting started

Plenty of cafes wind down around midnight. Fernway by Stories stays open every day from 1pm – 6am, which makes it one of the few places on the Bangalore–Mysore Expressway where you can sit down for a proper meal, a coffee or a drink long after the city has gone quiet.

We call ourselves a cafe because that is how most people search for a late-night spot — but Fernway is a full garden restaurant, with a kitchen that keeps cooking through the night.

What midnight dining looks like here

Open-air garden tables, warm lights, candlelit tables every night and a menu built for long conversations.

  • North Indian favourites like chatpata chicken tikka and paneer tikka
  • Global comfort food, small plates and quick bites
  • Desserts, coffee, cold brews and frappes
  • Pet-friendly garden seating with space for groups

Where we are

Fernway is in Mayaganahalli, between Bidadi and Ramanagara, right on the Bangalore–Mysore Expressway (Mysore Road). Address: Q88J+78G, Mayaganahalli, Bengaluru, Karnataka 562128. It's an easy late-night drive out of Bengaluru, and an easy stop if you're driving back from Mysuru.

Planning a late night with friends? Book a table online or call 080-471-62244 — weekends fill fast.

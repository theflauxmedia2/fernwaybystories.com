Welcome

A Late-Night Garden Cafe & Restaurant on the Bangalore–Mysore Expressway

Fernway by Stories is a new iconic landmark in Mayaganahalli, on the Bengaluru Mysore Highway near Ramanagara — garden dining and open-air seating for afternoons and evenings that deserve more than ordinary. Candlelit tables, North Indian and globally inspired food, and coffee for conversations that run late — we're open till 6am. Pull up. Stay as long as you like.

Good to Know

Fernway by Stories FAQs

Where is Fernway by Stories located?

Fernway by Stories is in Mayaganahalli on the Bangalore Mysore Highway (Mysore Road), between Bidadi and Ramanagara. The full address is Q88J+78G, Mayaganahalli, Bengaluru, Karnataka 562128. It's an easy drive out of Bangalore city and a natural stop on any Bangalore–Mysore road trip.

What are Fernway by Stories timings?

We're open every day from 1pm – 6am, so you can stop by for lunch, dinner, or late-night plates and drinks under the open sky.

Is Fernway a late-night cafe?

Yes. Fernway is open every day from 1pm – 6am — one of the few late-night cafes on the Bangalore–Mysore Expressway. Drop in for midnight dining, late-night coffee and food after a long drive or a night out.

Which restaurants on the Bangalore–Mysore highway are open late at night?

Fernway by Stories in Mayaganahalli, between Bidadi and Ramanagara, is open every day from 1pm – 6am — so whether you want lunch, dinner or a 3am meal, there's a full garden cafe and restaurant open on the Bangalore–Mysore highway.

Is Fernway good for a date night or candlelight dinner?

Yes. Candlelit garden tables, greenery and live music make Fernway a favourite with couples for date nights and candlelight dinners — an easy drive out of Bengaluru, away from city noise.

What is the price for two at Fernway by Stories?

A meal for two at Fernway by Stories costs about ₹800, which makes it an affordable garden cafe and restaurant stop on the Bangalore–Mysore highway — whether for lunch, dinner or a late-night meal.

How do I book a table at Fernway by Stories?

Reserve online through our table booking link, call the Fernway by Stories contact number 080-471-62244, or message us on WhatsApp. Weekends fill fast, so we recommend booking ahead.

What food does Fernway by Stories serve?

Our menu brings together North Indian favourites like chatpata chicken tikka and paneer tikka, global comfort food, veg and non-veg starters, small plates and quick bites, desserts, coffee, cold brews and frappes.

Is Fernway good for families and groups?

Yes. Spacious garden seating makes Fernway one of the most relaxed family restaurants on the Bangalore Mysore Highway — ideal for family lunches, family dinners, group dining with friends, and team dinners. We're pet friendly too.

Does Fernway by Stories have live music?

Yes. Every Friday we host a candlelight midnight jamming session from 11pm to 2am, and every Saturday a live band plays in our open-air garden. We also run DJ and lounge nights and themed evenings — follow us on Instagram or see our events page for what's coming up.

Can I celebrate a birthday or anniversary at Fernway?

Absolutely. Fernway is a popular venue for birthday dinners, anniversary dinners, date nights, and private celebrations. Share your plans through our event enquiry form and our team will help with menus and seating.
